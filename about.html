About The Daily Summit

A free daily trivia game about the answers other people give.

How it works

Every day has seven questions, such as "Name something you'd bring on a road trip." Name an answer to climb. Rare answers climb higher (10, 25, 50 or 100 feet each), but name one of the most common answers and you lose that round's climb. Bank your height at any camp, and see how high you get on the ranks ladder.

Everyone gets the same seven questions on a given day. Play unlimited routes for practice, share a result with a friend, or start a room to compare scores with your group.

About the answer rankings

How common an answer is has been estimated by an AI-assisted process, not measured in a real survey. The rankings are meant to be fun and fair, but they are not perfect. If a reasonable answer is rejected, it simply isn't on the board yet and it costs you nothing.

Privacy

  • No accounts and no ads. You never sign up or log in.
  • Anonymous play stats. The game sends a random ID stored in your browser along with what you played and scored, so the answer rankings can improve. We do not store your IP address, name or email. The only location kept is a two-letter country code.
  • On your device. Your best score, daily result, streak and settings are saved in your browser's local storage and stay there.
  • Friend rooms. If you create or join a room, the nickname you type and your daily scores are stored so the room's leaderboard can show them to people who have the room's code. Do not use your real name if you would rather not. Leaving a room just stops sending new scores.
  • Challenge links. A link only contains a score, rank, result colors and an optional name. It never contains the questions or your answers.
